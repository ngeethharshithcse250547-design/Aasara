# Competitor Analysis - PS 26092

**Comparison focus:** myScheme (Government of India) and the proposed NSFDC-focused prototype  
**Reviewed:** 9 September 2026

## Executive conclusion

myScheme is already a strong government-scheme discovery platform. It offers personalised discovery, eligibility checks, scheme detail pages, application guidance and a voice-enabled multilingual AI chatbot. The SIH product should not claim that AI, voice, or generic government-scheme discovery is novel.

The credible differentiation is a narrower, action-oriented financial-assistance journey for NSFDC users:

> **Potential fit -> financial understanding -> application readiness -> accountable channel-partner routing.**

## Verified myScheme capabilities

myScheme's official FAQ states that it:

- is a national discovery platform spanning Central and State/UT schemes;
- uses demographic, income, social-category and other profile data for personalised search;
- provides scheme-specific eligibility questionnaires;
- provides required-document information and application guidance;
- currently redirects the user to the concerned Ministry/Department application page; and
- has a voice-enabled, multilingual AI chatbot with voice and text interaction, Hindi/English support, context-aware conversations and session-based history.

These are capabilities to respect, not features to rebrand as original.

## Feature comparison

| Journey need | myScheme today | Proposed PS 26092 MVP | Differentiation claim that is supportable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad scheme discovery | Central and State/UT discovery with search and filters. | Start with five NSFDC credit products. | Focus and depth, not breadth. |
| Personalised eligibility | Profile-led search and scheme-specific questionnaire. | Deterministic NSFDC rule checks with reasons. | Make the financial matching logic and unresolved conditions visible. |
| Voice / chatbot | Voice-enabled multilingual AI chatbot, Hindi/English and text support. | Optional accessibility layer for profile capture. | Do not call voice the USP. |
| Scheme detail | Benefits, criteria, FAQs and application details. | Published caps, rates, terms, calculator assumptions and match evidence. | Financial consequences are made clearer for the chosen need. |
| Documents | Required documents for schemes. | A user-owned readiness checklist: present / missing / scheme-specific. | Distinguish possible eligibility from readiness to apply. |
| Application | Redirects to the relevant application page. | PM-SURAJ/offline route plus verified category/partner directory. | The next physical/digital route is made explicit. |
| Partner routing | Not claimed as a core published capability in reviewed FAQ. | NSFDC directory records, category fit and geography. | Directly implements the PS's geo-spatial channel-partner requirement. |
| Partner financial status | No claim assessed. | Only official/authorised feed; otherwise no claim. | Trustworthy data boundaries are a feature. |

## Product positioning

### One sentence

A multilingual, explainable assistant that helps an eligible SC beneficiary understand which NSFDC finance product may fit, what repayment could look like, what to prepare, and where to take the next official step.

### What not to say

- "We are the first AI platform for government schemes."
- "We solve scheme discovery with voice."
- "Our AI approves eligibility."
- "Our map shows live funding availability" without an authorised live feed.

### What to say instead

- "We specialise the NSFDC financing journey after discovery."
- "Our recommendation is rule-based, explainable and source-versioned."
- "We make eligibility, readiness and the next route visible in one flow."
- "We keep verified directory facts separate from simulated demo status."

## Build implications

1. **Do not overbuild a general scheme catalogue.** The MVP should be five NSFDC products with auditable fields.
2. **Lead the demo with a financial need.** Example: a beneficiary seeks INR 1 lakh for a tailoring business, then sees a potential MFS match, quarterly estimate, checklist and relevant official route.
3. **Keep AI bounded.** It may translate voice/text into profile fields; reviewed rules decide the recommendation.
4. **Show data provenance.** Every result should expose a source date and official link.
5. **Use partner status carefully.** Named partner addresses can be verified from the official directory. Availability, utilisation and financial health must remain blank unless supplied by a live, authorised source.

## Competitor gap hypothesis to validate

This is an MVP design hypothesis, not a statement that myScheme lacks these features in every context:

| Hypothesis | How to validate before final pitch |
|---|---|
| A beneficiary needs loan-specific repayment clarity after finding a scheme. | Test the prototype with 3-5 prospective users; capture whether rate, tenure and instalment frequency change understanding. |
| Document readiness reduces incomplete or misdirected applications. | Ask users to complete the checklist and record missing evidence categories. |
| Channel routing is valuable when NSFDC does not lend directly. | Test whether users can identify a correct next official route after using the directory. |
